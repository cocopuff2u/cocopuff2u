My Story

I’m a Senior Systems Endpoint Engineer at Wave Money Mobile, where I focus on securing and managing endpoints at scale. I’ve stepped out of the government space after years supporting the U.S. Navy as a civilian, but not away from the mission: getting Macs into the most demanding environments, from high-compliance organizations to the labs and research teams pushing science forward.

I’m the sole developer of the open-source project M.A.C.E. (Mac Advanced Compliance Editor), the maintainer of MOFA (Microsoft Overview Feed for Apple), and an active contributor to the mSCP government working group. My goal is simple: I want to make tools for people like me, the admins in the trenches, that make their lives easier and their environments more secure. We impact users more than most people realize. Our decisions determine whether Apple stays or goes, whether a system is secure or not, whether compliance is a burden or a baseline. So why shouldn’t it be easy for both us and the end user? That’s what drives everything I build.

With experience spanning higher education, government, fintech, and high-compliance environments, I build for every admin who’s ever felt the weight of keeping Apple secure at scale. You can find all of my projects in one place at CoCoLabs and on my GitHub.

Outside of work, I enjoy gaming, connecting with my community, and exploring Charleston, SC. I also have a deep love for outer space and hope to one day work with NASA or in a role that supports humanity’s exploration beyond Earth. It’s the nerd in me that yearns for the stars and believes they may one day be our next home.

Featured Projects

M.A.C.E.

Mac Advanced Compliance Editor
   

M.A.C.E. is a macOS app for Mac admins to easily create, document, and audit compliance baselines using NIST's mSCP 2.0 with visual rule editing and flexible management.

Check it out!

MOFA Website

   

I developed MOFA (Microsoft Overview Feed for Apple) using Vue.js and Python, hosted on GitHub Pages, to provide Mac administrators with automated, up-to-date feeds, tools, and direct download links for Microsoft Office apps on macOS.

Check it out!

BOFA Website

   

I built BOFA (Browser Overview Feed for Apple) with Vue.js and Python, hosted on GitHub Pages, to provide up-to-date browser feeds for Mac admins. It streamlines deployment with reliable XML, JSON, and YAML data.

Check it out!

mSCP Contributor

 

I help build the website for NIST's macOS Security Compliance Project (mSCP), the open-source framework for macOS security baselines, and actively contribute to the community that maintains it.

Check it out!

MacOS Admin Scripts

 

A collection of scripts for Mac admins, built to deploy through MDMs like Jamf, Intune and Kandji, with user-friendly tools like an App Uninstaller and Network Health Check.
